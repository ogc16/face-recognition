# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and safe for
everyone, regardless of experience, age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before making assumptions.
- Critique code, ideas, and decisions, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never request or publish real face images, embeddings,
  attendance records, or other personal data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions on scope and security trade-offs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats,
personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information, and deliberate
disruption of discussions or reviews are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project, as
well as when an individual is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the
project maintainers through GitHub. Do not include biometric data or other
sensitive personal information in a public report. The maintainers will review
reports confidentially, respond as appropriate, and respect the privacy and
safety of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently ban participants when behavior violates this Code of Conduct. They
will make decisions fairly and will prioritize the safety and privacy of the
affected people.
